### Lab 8: Advanced FIM Architectures — SON Partitioning, AClose & Pincer Search
- **Directory:** [`c:/Users/Sreekar_Tirumala/Documents/College/Big Data/lab8`](file:///c:/Users/Sreekar_Tirumala/Documents/College/Big%20Data/lab8)
- **Key Files:**

  - [`FIM_Algorithms_Test.ipynb`](file:///c:/Users/Sreekar_Tirumala/Documents/College/Big%20Data/lab8/FIM_Algorithms_Test.ipynb): Verification test suite comparing all four algorithms on synthetic and FoodMart data.
  - [`FIM_Lab_Report.docx`](file:///c:/Users/Sreekar_Tirumala/Documents/College/Big%20Data/lab8/FIM_Lab_Report.docx): Formal experimental report.
- **Algorithms:**
  - SON Partitioning (Savasere et al.): Guarantees 2 passes via local support $minsup \times |P_i|$ across non-overlapping slices.
  - AClose (Pasquier et al.): Computes Frequent Closed Itemsets (FCIs) and minimal generators using closure operator $h(I) = \bigcap_{t \in \mathcal{T}(I)} t$.
  - Pincer Search (Lin & Dunham): Bidirectional bottom-up candidate generation combined with top-down pruning via Maximum Frequent Candidate Set (MFCS) to mine Maximal Frequent Itemsets (MFIs).
- **Inputs:** Synthetic transaction list and FoodMart transactions.
- **Outputs:** Closed itemset maps, generator mappings, maximal frequent itemset dictionaries.
